Mission & responsibilities

You will build the financial-control backbone of a growing SpaceTech company, from a public-contract-compliant cost-center set-up to the monthly close.

Financial control & reporting

  1. Own cost-center accounting and financial reporting compliant with government and other public contracts
  2. Prepare the monthly and quarterly close and management reporting, and input for investor reporting
  3. Maintain plan-vs-actual tracking across departments

Compliance & governance

  1. Ensure financial reporting complies with German HGB and government-contract-specific requirements
  2. Support the annual audit and tax filing, working with our external tax advisor

Cross-functional partnership

  1. Partner with our Program & Contracts Manager on milestone-payment invoicing and revenue recognition
  2. Support the CEO and VP Operations on cash-flow planning

What we are looking for

Must have

  • 8+ years in financial controlling or accounting, ideally with exposure to government/defence-funded or other milestone-based revenue models
  • Strong working knowledge of German HGB accounting and cost-center/project accounting
  • Comfortable being the company’s first dedicated finance hire and building processes from scratch
  • Fluent German and English

Nice to have

  • Experience with international group structures (e.g. subsidiaries and intercompany invoicing)
  • Experience with the accounting and ERP tooling of a small, fast-growing company
Eligibility

Willingness and eligibility to undergo a security clearance check (Sicherheitsüberprüfung, SÜG) may be required for this role, depending on project assignment.

About Starflight Dynamics

Starflight Dynamics (SFDY) is a Munich-based SpaceTech company building the foundational technologies for Europe's next generation of orbital infrastructure. Founded in 2023, we develop dual-use systems for space and defence: a green, water-based orbital propulsion stack and modular platforms for in-orbit operations.

Working with partners such as the University of the German Armed Forces in Munich and the BAAINBw, we're a small, ambitious team moving fast from prototype to orbit. Join us and help shape how Europe operates, and defends its interests, in space.
