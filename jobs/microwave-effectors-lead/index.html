Mission & responsibilities

You will build the technical foundation of our microwave effector capability from scratch, from first concepts towards an on-orbit payload.

Technical leadership

  1. Build the technical foundation for our microwave effector systems as the founding hire of the function
  2. Own delivery of microwave effector-system commitments
  3. Own the strategic direction of the function, including the vision for future products, technologies and projects
  4. Work closely with our Propulsion team on shared microwave sources

Technical direction

  1. Set the technical architecture and roadmap for microwave effector and payload systems
  2. Represent the function in company-level technical reviews
  3. Contribute to proposals that fund our activities

Hiring & team building

  1. Define hiring profiles and grow the team
  2. Establish engineering processes and documentation standards
  3. Be accountable for personnel safety and security in the function

What we are looking for

Must have

  • 5-10 years of technical leadership in RF/microwave, effector, and/or payload systems, including small-team leadership
  • Background in defence- and space-related technical and engineering work
  • Comfortable building a technical roadmap from zero and contributing to building a team
  • Able to run a close, dotted-line technical relationship with adjacent departments and share common technology bricks

Nice to have

  • Hands-on experience with high-power microwave sources (e.g. magnetron-based systems)
  • Experience writing or contributing to ESA, BAAINBw or comparable funding proposals
  • Experience developing or qualifying on-orbit payloads

About Starflight Dynamics

Starflight Dynamics (SFDY) is a Munich-based SpaceTech company building the foundational technologies for Europe's next generation of orbital infrastructure. Founded in 2023, we develop dual-use systems for space and defence: a green, water-based orbital propulsion stack and modular platforms for in-orbit operations.

Working with partners such as the University of the German Armed Forces in Munich and the BAAINBw, we're a small, ambitious team moving fast from prototype to orbit. Join us and help shape how Europe operates, and defends its interests, in space.
