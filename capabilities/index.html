Our platforms maintain proximity to other objects for inspection and space domain awareness. They combine commercial subsystems with our proprietary technology in a hardened design, producible at scale.

Concept development and mission preparation

Our simulation systems let space forces think through operations before they happen. Large forces use them to develop how they operate at scale; smaller forces, to see what they can achieve, alone and with partners.
